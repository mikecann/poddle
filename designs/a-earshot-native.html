Earshot for iPhone

What Apple might ship if it built a podcast app for people who listen at 1.8×. It sits right inside iOS 26: floating Liquid Glass tab bar and controls, large titles, continuous corners, grouped sheets. All the craft goes into the details: speed-adjusted times, a chapter-aware scrubber, and a fine-grained speed dial. One calm accent, a deep tide green, on warm paper-white. The colour mostly comes from the artwork, and Now Playing takes its tint from it. Motion should feel like the system: spring physics, sheets that follow your finger, rows that lift when you reorder them.
