EarfulA podcast player for Mike, built to replace Overcast

Earful should feel like a well-made radio on the kitchen bench. It's warm paper and deep ink, with a tomato needle for "now" and a gold pin wherever a show has its own settings. Headings and numbers use SF Pro Rounded and everything else uses SF Pro Text, with a receipt mono kept for the fun bits. Depth comes from soft shadows, pressed-in wells and a fine paper grain instead of glass. Controls move like hardware: the speed dial clicks in 0.05× detents with a haptic tick, sheets settle with a small spring, and buttons sink a point when you press them.
