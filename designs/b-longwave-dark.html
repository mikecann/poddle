Longwave

Longwave treats your podcasts like an instrument panel, not a media shelf. It's near-black (never pure black) with one signal colour, Signal Lime, kept for whatever is live: the play button, your speed, the time you've won back. Type is SF Pro at heavy weights with tabular figures, so speed, time left and stats read at a glance like a good fitness tracker. Motion is quick and physical: rows snap to their actions, sheets spring out of the control you touched, and numbers tick over rather than fade.

Colour

Lime appears only on live or "yours" things: play, your current speed, time saved, new episodes, overrides. Everything else is greyscale, so the accent always means something.

Numbers

Every time and speed uses tabular figures, with units set smaller and muted (8h 04m) so the digits carry the weight and don't jitter as they count.

Overrides

One marker system across the app: a lime speed tag in lists, a SHOW tag on Now Playing, and lime dots in settings for anything that differs from your defaults.

Motion

Springs with almost no bounce. Swipe actions snap at 50% travel, sheets grow out of the control that opened them, and readouts roll digit by digit when speed changes.
